About

A quiet way of looking.

Ronald Jefferson is a Bengaluru-based photographer working across travel, wildlife and portraiture. From Rajasthan’s forts and deserts to city streets at dusk, his work waits for the quiet in a scene — the light between moments. He shoots for people, brands and publications across India.
